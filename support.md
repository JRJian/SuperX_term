# SuperX Support

If you have questions, encounter a problem, or would like to share feedback about SuperX, please contact us through either of the following channels:

- **Email:** [szsb_jian@outlook.com](mailto:szsb_jian@outlook.com)
- **GitHub Issues:** [Open an issue in this repository](https://github.com/JRJian/SuperX_term/issues)

When reporting a problem, please include your SuperX version, device model, iOS version, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue. Screenshots may also help us understand the problem.

GitHub issues are public. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.
